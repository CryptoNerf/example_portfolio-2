Gesture Guide
By Alexanyan Emile

CREATE MOLECULE

Gesture: Pinch gesture
How to perform: Bring your thumb and index finger together (pinch position) and hold for 1 second.
→ Creates a new molecule at the pinch position
[Place image: pinch_gesture.gif]

EDIT MODE

Gesture: Open mouth
How to perform: Open your mouth wide to activate edit mode (✎).
→ Switches to EDIT MODE (✎) for molecule manipulation
[Place image: open_mouth.gif]

MOVE MOLECULE

Gesture: Palm (open hand)
How to perform: Show your open palm and move it around to reposition molecules.
→ Moves the selected molecule to palm position
[Place image: palm_move.gif]

SIZE & SHAPE

Gesture: Palm in edit mode (✎)
How to perform: In edit mode, use your palm width to control size and bend fingers to change shape.

• Palm width = molecule size
• Bent fingers = molecule shape
→ Adjusts molecule size and shape dynamically
[Place image: palm_edit.gif]

LOCK MOLECULE

Gesture: All fingers together
How to perform: Close all fingers together (fist position) and hold for 1 second.
→ Locks the selected molecule (prevents movement)
[Place image: lock_single.gif]

LOCK ALL

Gesture: Both hands, fingers together
How to perform: Close all fingers on both hands together and hold for 1 second.
→ Locks ALL molecules on the canvas
[Place image: lock_all.gif]

UNLOCK

Gesture: Pinky finger
How to perform: Extend your pinky finger while keeping other fingers closed.
→ Unlocks locked molecules
[Place image: unlock.gif]

DELETE MOLECULE

Gesture: Crossed fingers
How to perform: Cross your index and middle fingers and hold for 1 second.
→ Deletes the selected molecule
[Place image: delete.gif]

SOUND DEFORMATION

Gesture: Voice/Sound input
How to perform: Make sounds or speak near the microphone to create audio-reactive deformations.
→ Deforms molecule contours based on sound frequency
[Place image: sound.gif]

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Ctrl+C → Copy selected molecule
Ctrl+V → Paste copied molecule
Ctrl+Z → Undo last action
→ Quick actions using keyboard
[Place image: keyboard.png]
