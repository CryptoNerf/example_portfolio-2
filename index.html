If you found your way here by accident, wandering through the internet,
that’s okay — it’s part of the idea. By being here, you’re already
involved. You’re now looking at the archive of my work. I’m Dasha Mamba,
and this text is mine.

My practice explores how we perceive space and how space, in turn,
affects us. I work with sound, light, color, scent, touch, and taste —
using multiple senses to understand how inner experience is formed and how
it changes in different environments. By creating immersive spaces, I try
to sharpen perception and invite people to look more closely at their own
sensations and emotions.
